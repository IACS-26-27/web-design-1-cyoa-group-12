Just Go to Waterfall

You have forgotten your map and end up at a fork in the trail. You have walked 1 mile so far and remember that on this trail there is only one turn on the path to the waterfall. There are 2 options: a left path that seems to be mostly flat and curves in about 100 feet and with the thick trees you can’t tell what the trail looks after that. The right path is a downward slope with logs blocking the path and looks a little overgrown. Which way should you go?
