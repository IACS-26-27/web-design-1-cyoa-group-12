The Camping trip

You're on a camping trip with your friends in thanksgiving forest on a humid July weekend. It’s mid afternoon when you arrive at the campsite for the night. a clearing ~4 miles away from the parking lot at the base of a rocky cliff around 20ft tall. The site is covered by trees and the ground is somewhat clear of rocks. There is a trail off your campsite about 3 miles long that supposedly leads to a beautiful waterfall. You and your friends should set up the campsite and put your food in the bear box but you all want to go see the waterfall before it gets dark.

Set up camp then go to waterfall or Go to waterfall then set up when you get back
