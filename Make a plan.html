After taking some time to grab your map and some snacks and water you set off for the waterfall. The path is hilly with rocks and logs on the path but you navigate them easily. As you come over the last hill the waterfall comes into view. The hike takes about an hour. It is just as beautiful as it was described with the sunshine reflecting off the water making it sparkle. It even has a swimming hole at the top of the falls which looks to be about 6 ft deep and after a hike in the hot humid forest a swim sounds quite refreshing. The group sits down and eats a quick snack well enjoying the sound of the waterfall. Some of your group wants to swim and some of you think you should head back to camp before it gets too dark.
