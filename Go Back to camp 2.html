Go back to camp

As you round the turn to go back to your camp your bags are not where you left them. All if your food is torn open and spread across the campsite. The backpacks are ripped and pieces of them are spread across the site. Everyone starts running around searching and taking in how much damage you have to deal with. It is very clear some animal was there and the animal has destroyed the chance you had for a nice camping trip. You slowly start to gather whatever stuff you can grab and make the defeated hike back to the cars. The End.
