Left Path

You have chosen the left path. The path slopes up and down over small hills, twists left and right around trees and huge boulders. As you are walking the sun is setting, the forest is growing darker and the path is almost entirely in shadow. The carefree attitude you all had not long ago is starting to fade away just like the light and heat the sun was giving your group. You and the group decide to stop and take a break to figure out the next step in a small clearing with a few paths branching off of it. It is becoming very clear to your group that you have taken the wrong path and you have no idea where you are. It is also becoming clear that you don’t have enough time before the sunset to make it back to camp let alone the waterfall. You are lost in the woods with no chance of getting back to your camp before it gets dark. Stranded in the middle of the forest your group starts gathering sticks and logs to build a fire and a small shelter so you can hopefully survive the night. The end.
