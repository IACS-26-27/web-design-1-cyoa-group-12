Right path

The path goes on a downward slope for about 15 minutes with you and your friends tripping over logs on the way down. It starts to slope up slowly and as it does the sounds in the forest start to shift from birds chirping to water slashing on to rocks. As you go over the top of the hill the waterfall comes into view below you. It is just as beautiful as you expect it to be with the sunshine reflecting off the water making it sparkle. It even has a swimming hole at the top of the falls which looks to be about 6 ft deep and after a hike in the hot humid forest a swim sounds quite refreshing. Some off the group wants to go for a swim though others think you should go back so you can get camp set up before dark.
