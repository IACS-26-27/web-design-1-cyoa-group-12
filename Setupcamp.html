Set up camp

To set up camp you have to complete alot of things to make sure the camp is safe

Camp Set up Checklist
  • Clear ground for rocks
  • Set up tents
  • Put food away
  • Gather firewood
  • Set up fire spot

You and your friend decide to split up the checklist and each help get a few things done. Setting up takes about an hour with everyone helping so you still have about 4 hours of sunlight left in the day. That should be enough to make it to the waterfall and back but you don’t have flashlights with you so if you get caught in the dark your in trouble. Should you

Stay at camp or Risk it and go to waterfall
